01 · Profile

A multidisciplinary approach to legal work

Avinash holds a BA LL.B. from the National University of Study and Research in Law (NUSRL) and an LL.M. in Corporate & Commercial Law. He is UGC-NET (Law) qualified, with eligibility for Assistant Professor.

He currently serves as Administrative Officer (Legal) at The New India Assurance Co. Ltd., handling the legal and strategic aspects of Motor Third-Party Claims, including MACT litigation, liability assessment and insurance-related legal matters. He previously held the same role at National Insurance Company Limited.

Before insurance, he worked in research and strategic communication, and in content strategy for large organisations, including the Adani Group and pharmaceutical companies. He brings legal expertise, research, strategic thinking and clear communication to complex professional and organisational problems.
